The Four Nations

Each nation shaped its people, its homes and its way of bending.

Air Nomads

Four temples, high in the peaks

Monks who live light, move freely and rarely stay in one place. Their airbenders are gone, but Aang carries on their ways.

Water Tribes

Poles of ice, a spirit oasis

Healers and fierce defenders who follow the moon. Waterbending flows with the tides and heals what is hurt.

Earth Kingdom

Walled cities and wide farmland

The largest nation, built on patience and strength. Earthbenders hold their ground and never back down.

Fire Nation

Volcanic islands of the east

A proud nation of ambition and power. Firebending burns from breath and will, and it can create as well as destroy.
